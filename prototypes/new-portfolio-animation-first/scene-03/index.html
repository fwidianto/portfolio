THE PROJECT / SERVICE DIVISION

I did not just process requests.
I helped build a better process.

At PT Traktor Nusantara, I helped centralize purchase request and purchase proposal processing for the Service Division. Branch submissions needed a consistent path into headquarters review and management approval.

My contribution

I helped build a lightweight internal workflow with Google Forms for structured intake, Google Sheets and formulas for centralized tracking and control, and Apps Script for automation support. It supported request IDs, document checks, status updates and review preparation.

Where judgment remained

HQ still checked supporting documents, business reasons and the related customer or job context. Manager review and general manager approval remained human decisions. Automation supported administration and visibility.

This is an illustrative reconstruction based on the owner-provided account. All 15 branch names follow the Chapter III Scene 01 network; Jakarta is Head Office. The issue assigned to each branch is illustrative, not a historical finding about that office. Request IDs, amounts, rows, field layouts and timing are synthetic. The interface is not a copy of the historical system. No confidential records are used. This was not a full ERP replacement, automatic purchase approval or automatic SAP PO generation. No quantified savings are claimed.
