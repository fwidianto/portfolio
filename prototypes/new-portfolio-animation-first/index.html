Engineering Process Explainer

How the Palm Oil Milling Process Works

PT Astra Agro Lestari · Central Kalimantan

This is a simplified view of the palm oil milling process observed during my internship at PT Astra Agro Lestari.

01

Fresh Fruit Bunches (FFB) Intake

Raw Material Intake
What Happens

Harvested palm fruit bunches arrive from plantation estates and are weighed, inspected, and unloaded onto ramps to feed the processing line.

Why It Matters

Freshness is critical. Processing fruit promptly after harvesting prevents the oil from degrading and becoming acidic.

02

Sterilization

Thermal Softening
What Happens

Fruit bunches enter large autoclave vessels and are cooked with high-pressure steam.

Why It Matters

Heat stops natural enzymes from spoiling the oil, softens the fruit tissue for pressing, and loosens fruitlets from the stalks.

03

Threshing

Fruitlet Separation
What Happens

The softened bunches tumble inside a rotating drum, separating loose fruitlets from empty bunch stalks.

Why It Matters

Isolates the oil-bearing fruit from bulky bunch waste. The empty stalks are collected and reused as biomass boiler fuel or organic mulch.

04

Pressing

Mechanical Oil Extraction
What Happens

The fruit is mashed and squeezed through continuous screw presses, separating crude oil liquor from solid press cake.

Why It Matters

Extracts the crude oil mechanically without crushing the hard palm kernels. The remaining fiber and shell by-products are reused as boiler fuel to generate steam and electricity for the mill.

05

Clarification

Density-Driven Separation
What Happens

The pressed oil mixture enters settling tanks where gravity naturally separates the pure, lighter oil from heavier water, sand, and sludge.

Why It Matters

Purifies the crude palm oil cleanly through natural density differences without chemical additives.

06

CPO Storage

Stabilization & Dispatch
What Happens

The clarified oil is dried and pumped into bulk storage tanks, maintained at controlled temperature until dispatched for transport.

Why It Matters

Keeping moisture low prevents oil spoilage during holding, ensuring commodity Crude Palm Oil meets quality standards for refinery dispatch.

2019

My undergraduate thesis was about seawater distillation using throttling and flash evaporation.

I built and tested a physical rig in the Mechanical Engineering laboratory at Universitas Indonesia, then compared the experiment with the thermodynamic model behind it.

A lot of the work was not glamorous—setting up the rig, adjusting valves, waiting for temperature and pressure to stabilize, collecting the distillate, and repeating the test when something did not behave as expected.

It became the last big project of my university years before I graduated in 2019.
