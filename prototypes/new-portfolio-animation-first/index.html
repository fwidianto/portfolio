Engineering Process Explainer

How the Palm Oil Milling Process Works

Continuous Thermal, Mechanical & Density-Driven Separation

“The mill operates as an interconnected thermodynamic and mechanical system: rapidly deactivating enzymes with steam, extracting crude fluid under progressive screw pressure, and separating oil from sludge via density differentials.”

01

Fresh Fruit Bunches (FFB) Intake

Raw Material Delivery & Ramp Feeding
What Happens

Harvested oil palm fruit bunches arrive from plantation estates by tipper trucks and rail carts, discharging into receiving hoppers and loading ramps for initial inspection and continuous batch feeding.

Why It Matters

Freshness dictates end-product quality. Lipase enzymes inside damaged fruit begin converting triglycerides into Free Fatty Acids (FFA) immediately upon harvesting; rapid delivery and uninterrupted intake ensure high oil recovery and prevent acidification.

02

In-Line Weighing

Tonnage Measurement & Mass Accounting
What Happens

Each delivery cart and vehicle crosses high-precision weighbridge scale platforms equipped with shear-beam load cells, recording gross intake mass against calibrated tare weights.

Why It Matters

Accurate raw tonnage provides the essential baseline denominator for the entire plant’s mass balance. Oil Extraction Rate (OER) calculations and processing throughput targets directly rely on this initial measurement.

03

High-Pressure Sterilization

Thermal Enzyme Deactivation & Tissue Softening
What Happens

Fruit bunches enter cylindrical horizontal autoclave vessels, where they undergo multi-peak saturated steam injection (2.5–3.0 bar) for thorough thermal conditioning followed by controlled blowdown and discharge.

Why It Matters

High-temperature steam permanently deactivates lipase enzymes (halting FFA rise), softens cellular mesocarp tissue to release oil droplets, and loosens individual fruitlets from the fibrous stalk for mechanical stripping.

04

Twin-Screw Pressing

Mechanical Compression & Slurry Expulsion
What Happens

Cooked mash drops into a twin-screw press. Counter-rotating tapered augers compress the mass against a perforated press cage, extruding crude slurry while conveying solid fiber and nuts axially toward the discharge cone.

Why It Matters

Progressive mechanical compression expels up to 90% of the oil-water slurry without fracturing the inner palm nuts. Solid press cake is recovered as boiler fuel (mesocarp fiber) and palm kernel raw material.

05

Settling Clarification

Density Stratification & Gravity Separation
What Happens

The hot crude slurry is pumped into a vertical continuous settling clarifier. Thermal retention and quiet laminar conditions allow gravity to stratify the multi-phase mixture into distinct density layers.

Why It Matters

Heavier water, sand, and colloidal sludge settle downward into the conical sump, while lighter golden oil floats to the top, cleanly overflowing a perimeter skimming weir for final water removal.

06

Clarified CPO Outflow & Storage

Moisture Removal & Bulk Commodity Transfer
What Happens

Skimmed oil undergoes vacuum drying to remove residual moisture before pumping into insulated bulk storage tanks monitored by continuous sight gauges and temperature transmitters.

Why It Matters

Lowering moisture below 0.15% prevents hydrolytic rancidity and bacterial degradation during prolonged storage and transport, yielding stable industrial Crude Palm Oil (CPO) ready for refinery dispatch.
