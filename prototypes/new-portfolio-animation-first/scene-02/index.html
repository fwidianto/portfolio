THE PROJECT / 2019—2022

A practical improvement
to everyday operations.

As a Cost Control Analyst at PT Traktor Nusantara, I was assigned to help improve the fuel payment process for our service fleet. Mechanics had been advancing personal money, collecting receipts, and submitting reimbursement claims.

My contribution

I researched vendors, evaluated options, contacted Pertamina Retail, and helped establish the arrangement. I then monitored vehicle fuel usage, reviewed balance requirements, and recommended top-ups.

Technology & attribution

Pertamina Retail supplied the RFID cards and transaction reporting system. This project involved process improvement and implementation coordination. It did not include a custom real-time API, GPS tracking, or automatic account replenishment.

All vehicles, amounts, odometer readings, dates, interfaces, station geometry, and card artwork in the demonstration are illustrative. The approximate fleet size and monthly funding above are owner-supplied historical facts. No savings or fraud prevention results are claimed.
