BUSINESS CONTEXT

The issue was not just data. It was business visibility.

After ERP migration, standard Odoo reports helped with basic records, but management review still required connecting sales, purchasing, inventory, production, invoicing, and payment manually.

The work focused on turning scattered ERP transactions into validated, dashboard-ready views with clear source-of-truth logic.

Scattered transaction flow

Key review data sat across sales, purchasing, inventory, manufacturing, invoice, and payment records.

Manual review dependency

Users relied on ERP menu checks, spreadsheets, and process knowledge to answer follow-up questions.

Need for source-of-truth logic

Fields, statuses, custom references, and exceptions had to be validated before becoming KPI logic.
