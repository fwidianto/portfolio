Business Context

The problem was not missing data. It was a scattered business story.

One order could touch sales, purchasing, production, delivery, invoicing, and payment. The challenge was to connect those records into a flow that people could review and act on.

One order, many places

Sales, purchasing, production, delivery, invoicing, and payment records existed, but they were not easy to read as one business flow.
