Odoo ERP Analytics Platform

SQL analytics layer on Odoo PostgreSQL data for operational reporting, traceability, profitability review, and dashboard-ready business analysis.

Business Problem

Operational ERP data is useful only when it can be reviewed quickly and consistently across sales, purchasing, inventory, and profitability.

What It Provides

A public-safe analytics layer that organizes Odoo PostgreSQL data into reporting-ready views, traceability concepts, and decision-support summaries.

Public-Safe Scope

This page describes the analytics concept only. It does not expose customer, supplier, invoice, order, database, or credential details.

How It Helps

The focus is on helping business stakeholders review operational trends, compare profitability signals, and understand movement across ERP processes without relying on manual spreadsheet reconstruction.


  • Operational reporting for sales, purchasing, and inventory visibility
  • Traceability concepts for order flow and process review
  • Dashboard-ready summaries for management reporting
  • Cleaner handoff between ERP data and business analysis
