BUSINESS CONTEXT

The issue was not just data. It was business visibility.

After ERP migration, standard Odoo reporting was useful but not enough to answer practical management questions quickly. Operational review often required checking several ERP menus, comparing transaction flows manually, and interpreting how sales, procurement, inventory, production, invoicing, and payment records connected to each other.

The challenge was not only technical extraction. The real challenge was understanding how ERP transactions represented actual business processes, identifying the source of truth, validating business rules, and turning scattered operational records into dashboard-ready views.

Scattered transaction flow

Operational information was spread across sales, procurement, inventory, manufacturing, invoicing, and payment records.

Manual review dependency

Management review often depended on manual checking, spreadsheet reconstruction, and individual process knowledge.

Need for source-of-truth logic

Before building dashboards, the ERP fields, statuses, custom references, and exceptions needed to be interpreted correctly.

APPROACH / WORKFLOW

From business questions to validated analytics logic

I approached the project from the business process first, then translated that understanding into data relationships, SQL logic, dashboard structure, and reviewable outputs.

01

Clarify the business question

Define what management needs to review: order status, source, fulfillment progress, billing, payment, and profitability signals.

02

Map the ERP flow

Understand how transactions move across sales, internal orders, procurement, manufacturing, stock movement, invoicing, and finance.

03

Validate the source of truth

Review field relationships, custom references, transaction statuses, exceptions, and business meaning before creating reporting logic.

04

Build analytics logic

Use PostgreSQL and SQL to structure reporting views that summarize operational activity into reusable dashboard datasets.

05

Design dashboard views

Organize information into familiar operational review views instead of overwhelming users with raw database complexity.

06

Review with business context

Use AI-supported tools to speed up iteration while keeping human judgment on business rules, data quality, and decision usefulness.

ARCHITECTURE / DATA FLOW

How ERP transactions become reviewable business visibility

The dashboard starts from Odoo transaction records, then applies SQL extraction, business-rule validation, analytics views, and reporting outputs for management review and operational follow-up.

01
Layer 01 Odoo ERP Source

Sales, internal orders, purchasing, manufacturing, inventory, invoices, and payments.

02
Layer 02 PostgreSQL / SQL Extraction

Read-only queries, company filtering, canceled-state exclusion, and reference mapping.

03
Layer 03 Business Logic & Validation

Source classification, relationship checks, status logic, exception handling, and profitability signals.

04
Layer 04 Analytics Views / Dashboard Data

Sales Order View, Internal Order View, Material Tracking, and Invoice & Payment View.

05
Layer 05 Dashboard + Excel Export

Searchable dashboards, filters, and spreadsheet-friendly export for familiar review workflows.

06
Layer 06 Management Review / Follow-up

Supports management review, follow-up actions, issue checks, and cross-functional coordination.

Business value

From manual checking to reusable operational visibility

Before

  • Manual checking across multiple ERP menus
  • Order, procurement, delivery, invoice, and payment information felt scattered
  • Traceability depended heavily on individual knowledge
  • Reporting required repeated spreadsheet reconstruction
  • Business rules and exceptions were difficult to review consistently

After

  • Centralized dashboard views for operational review
  • Clearer connection across order flow and supporting transactions
  • Reusable SQL logic and dashboard-ready analytical structure
  • Faster review path for management reporting and follow-up
  • Better documentation of source-of-truth logic and data exceptions
