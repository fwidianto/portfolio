Business Reporting Automation

Excel, Google Sheets, Apps Script, VBA, UiPath, Looker Studio, and Python used to reduce manual reporting work and improve business review cadence.

Business Problem

Management reporting can become slow and inconsistent when it depends on repeated manual spreadsheet updates and copy-paste handoffs.

Method

Combine spreadsheet automation, scripted refresh steps, and structured dashboard outputs so reporting is easier to repeat and review.

Business Value

Improve visibility, reduce repetitive work, and create a more reliable path from raw data to management-ready reporting.

Typical Use Cases

  • Recurring report refreshes from spreadsheet and dashboard inputs
  • Executive summary packs prepared from structured business data
  • Manual workflow reduction using Apps Script, VBA, or UiPath
  • Operational and profitability reporting for internal review

Tools

  • Google Sheets
  • Looker Studio
  • Apps Script
  • VBA
  • UiPath
