Option 1 — Recommended

Build Journey + Architecture Stack

This is the strongest option. It shows both the problem-solving journey and the technical layers without making the reader feel like they are reading documentation.

Build Journey

From messy ERP flow to review-ready visibility

01

Messy ERP Flow

Sales, procurement, manufacturing, delivery, invoice, and payment records were connected in practice, but not easy to review in one place.

02

Source Mapping

SO, IO, PO, MO, DO, invoice, and payment relationships were mapped into a reviewable business flow.

03

Business Rule Validation

Fields, statuses, references, and exceptions were checked before becoming dashboard logic.

04

Review Logic

Validated rules were translated into SQL views, dashboard tables, export outputs, and follow-up signals.

05

Public-Safe Evidence

The portfolio version uses sanitized visuals and recreated examples to explain the work without exposing confidential data.
