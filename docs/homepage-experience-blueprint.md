# Homepage Experience Blueprint

Status: Living design document  
Owner: Fauzan Widianto

Purpose: Core memory for future homepage development

---

## Why This Document Exists

This document exists to guide future homepage development before jumping into design, animation, or implementation.

The homepage should not behave like an online resume or a static list of projects. It should behave like an experience that naturally shows how Fauzan thinks, learns, and solves business problems.

Before adding a new section, animation, interaction, or visual effect, future development should ask:

- Who will see this?
- What are they likely feeling or questioning?
- What should they understand without reading too much?
- What should they remember after leaving?
- Does this section help tell the portfolio story more clearly?

The goal is not just to make the homepage look better. The goal is to make the homepage feel memorable, active, and personally credible.

---

## Core Portfolio Memory

If visitors remember only one thing, it should be this:

> Fauzan enjoys untangling complex business systems and turning them into review-ready visibility.

Everything on the homepage should reinforce this idea.

---

## Audience

### 1. Recruiter / HR

Likely questions:

- What does this person actually do?
- Does this fit the role we are hiring for?
- Is this portfolio worth spending more time on?
- Is the profile clear enough to forward to a hiring manager?

Desired feeling:

> I can quickly understand his profile, and there is enough proof to continue.

---

### 2. Hiring Manager

Likely questions:

- Can he solve messy operational problems?
- Does he understand business systems before building dashboards?
- Can he work across operations, sales, procurement, finance, and management?
- Can I trust him with business reporting and process visibility?

Desired feeling:

> This person understands operations before technology.

---

### 3. Technical Reviewer / Senior Analyst

Likely questions:

- Is this real work or only a polished page?
- Does he understand data relationships?
- Can he explain technical work in business language?
- Is AI only being used as a shortcut, or is there human-led judgment behind the work?

Desired feeling:

> He understands both systems and implementation.

---

## Desired Emotional Journey

### First 10 seconds

The visitor should feel:

> I immediately understand the kind of work this portfolio is about.

The page should quickly communicate business systems, operations analytics, ERP, dashboards, and review visibility without requiring long reading.

---

### First 30 seconds

The visitor should feel:

> This person has worked with real operational systems.

The homepage should show enough business context to feel grounded, not just decorative or tool-focused.

---

### First minute

The visitor should feel:

> I want to open the Odoo case study.

The Odoo case study should remain the flagship proof because it best demonstrates the full workflow: messy ERP flow, mapping, validation, dashboard logic, review signals, and public-safe explanation.

---

### After opening a case study

The visitor should feel:

> He does not just build dashboards. He translates complex business systems into management visibility.

---

### Before leaving

The visitor should feel:

> I would like to talk to this person.

The homepage should make the CV, LinkedIn, GitHub, and contact path clear without feeling pushy.

## Homepage Story Structure

The homepage should gradually tell three connected stories.

### 1. Past — How I Learned

This should not be a plain work-history timeline.

It should show how Fauzan learned through real business problems:

- Cost control and SAP exposure
- Profitability and pricing review
- Manufacturing and business ownership exposure
- ERP migration and operational review
- Dashboard, SQL, Python, AI-assisted workflow, GitHub documentation, and portfolio building

The key message:

> The tools were learned because there were business problems to solve.

---

### 2. Present — What I Am Building Now

The homepage should feel alive.

It should show that Fauzan is actively building, improving, documenting, and learning.

Possible content:

- Currently improving portfolio UX
- Odoo ERP Analytics case study
- AI-assisted workflow and documentation
- Job search system / application tracking ideas
- Dashboard communication and review-signal design

The key message:

> This portfolio is active, not abandoned.

---

### 3. Future — What I Am Exploring

Not every valuable idea needs to be a finished project.

The homepage can show selected ideas to communicate initiative, curiosity, and systems thinking.

Possible content:

- Thinking Lab
- Ideas worth exploring
- Experiment backlog
- Future systems concepts

The key message:

> This person notices problems and thinks about systems to solve them.

## Priority Homepage Content

### 1. Learning Graph

Priority: Very high

Purpose:

Show continuous learning over multiple years.

This should not be a generic timeline of tools. It should show the relationship between:

- Year
- Business problem faced
- Capability developed
- Tools learned
- New way of thinking gained

Possible labels:

- Learning Graph
- How I Learned Through Problems
- From Mechanical Engineering to Business Systems Analytics
- Capability Growth Map

Possible visual style:

- Growing constellation
- Year-by-year capability graph
- Connected learning map
- Timeline with tool clusters
- Problem-to-capability flow

Content direction:

Each year or phase should answer:

- What problem did I face?
- What did I learn?
- What tools or systems did I use?
- How did my thinking evolve?

---

### 2. Currently Building

Priority: Very high

Purpose:

Show that the portfolio is actively maintained and that Fauzan is still learning and building.

Possible labels:

- Currently Building
- Active Workbench
- Now Improving
- Current Focus

Possible content:

- Portfolio experience design
- Odoo ERP Analytics page refinement
- AI-assisted development workflow
- CV / LinkedIn alignment
- Job hunting system
- Learning graph design

Design direction:

This section should feel light and current. It should avoid becoming a long update log.

The message should be:

> I am actively improving my work and learning in public-safe ways.

---

### 3. Thinking Lab

Priority: High

Purpose:

Show initiative and ideas without pretending every idea is already a finished project.

Possible labels:

- Thinking Lab
- Ideas I Am Exploring
- Systems I Want to Build
- Experiments

Possible content examples:

- Job Hunting OS
- Personal OS
- ERP Review Signals
- Portfolio Experience Design
- Meeting Intelligence
- AI Documentation Workflow
- Investment Decision Dashboard
- Dashboard UX simplification

Each idea should be short and structured:

- Problem
- Why it matters
- Possible direction
- Status

Important rule:

Only show curated ideas. Too many ideas can make the portfolio feel scattered.

Recommended limit:

- 5 to 8 ideas maximum on the homepage
- Deeper idea archive can be added later if needed

---

### 4. Flagship Odoo Proof

Priority: Keep strong

Purpose:

Show the strongest complete evidence of the portfolio story.

The Odoo case study should remain the flagship proof because it demonstrates:

- ERP complexity
- Business-rule validation
- Data relationship mapping
- Dashboard outputs
- Review signals
- Public-safe explanation
- AI-assisted but human-led development

The homepage should make visitors want to open it.

## Experience and Animation Ideas

Animation should tell a story, not decorate the page.

Possible homepage experience:

```text
Messy business records
→ connected relationships
→ validated logic
→ dashboard view
→ review signals
→ management visibility
```

Alternative experience:

```text
Sales Order
Purchase Order
Manufacturing Order
Delivery
Invoice
Payment

connect into

Review-ready dashboard
```

Alternative experience:

```text
Problem detected
→ find source
→ check relationship
→ validate rule
→ build review output
→ evidence complete
```

Rules:

- Keep animations subtle
- Avoid flashy SaaS-style motion
- Avoid heavy JavaScript
- Prefer short, meaningful, story-based movement
- The page should still work without animation

---

## What We Should Avoid

Avoid:

- Adding sections only because they look nice
- Showing every tool learned
- Making AI the main identity
- Long paragraphs
- Developer-heavy language
- Decorative-only animation
- Too many unfinished ideas
- Pretending all ideas are finished projects
- Fake metrics or unverified impact claims
- Confidential operational data

---

## Guiding Question

Every future homepage update should answer:

> Does this help visitors understand how Fauzan thinks?

If the answer is no, it probably does not belong on the homepage.
