## Purpose

Thinking Lab is a planned homepage section that shows how Fauzan thinks today.

If the Capability Graph answers:

> How did Fauzan become this person?

Then Thinking Lab answers:

> How does Fauzan think right now?

The section should show initiative, curiosity, and systems thinking without pretending that every idea is already a finished project.

---

## Why This Section Matters

Many valuable ideas begin before they become finished projects.

A portfolio that only shows completed work may hide the most interesting part of Fauzan's process:

- noticing friction
- asking questions
- imagining systems
- experimenting with tools
- connecting ideas across domains

Thinking Lab makes that process visible in a curated, professional way.

---

## Core Rule

Ideas should be framed as explorations.

Not achievements.

Do not overclaim unfinished work.

Do not make the portfolio feel scattered.

---

## Public Section Direction

Possible section label:

> Thinking Lab

Possible heading:

> Ideas I am exploring

Possible lead:

> Not every useful idea starts as a finished project. Some begin as questions about workflows, systems, and how information could be made easier to understand.

Alternative heading:

> Questions that may become systems

Alternative lead:

> My projects often begin as questions. This section collects selected ideas that are still being explored, tested, or shaped into future systems.

---

## Recommended Card Structure

Each idea card should include:

- Idea name
- Status
- Problem
- Why it matters
- Possible direction

Example format:

```text
Job Hunting OS
Status: Exploring
Problem: Applications, recruiter follow-ups, CV versions, and job recommendations are scattered.
Why it matters: Job searching becomes hard to review and improve without a system.
Possible direction: A lightweight tracker connected to Gmail, CV versions, and portfolio links.
```

## Candidate Ideas

### 1. Job Hunting OS

Status:

Exploring

Problem:

Applications, recruiter follow-ups, CV versions, and job recommendations are scattered.

Why it matters:

Job search performance is difficult to improve if the workflow is not tracked clearly.

Possible direction:

A lightweight system that connects application tracking, Gmail updates, CV versions, and job-fit notes.

---

### 2. Personal OS

Status:

Exploring

Problem:

Important learning, project context, decisions, and ideas can be lost across chats and documents.

Why it matters:

AI works better when context is structured and reusable.

Possible direction:

A GitHub or markdown-based knowledge system that stores project context, decisions, prompts, and personal working memory.

---

### 3. ERP Review Signals

Status:

Prototype / implemented in Odoo case study direction

Problem:

Dashboards often show many rows but do not clearly tell users what needs attention.

Why it matters:

Business users need review indicators, not only raw data.

Possible direction:

Operational review categories such as Healthy, Watchlist, Needs Review, Supplier Follow-up, and Operational Follow-up.

---

### 4. Portfolio Experience Design

Status:

Active

Problem:

A portfolio can become a static project list that does not explain how the person thinks.

Why it matters:

Visitors remember stories and experiences more than lists.

Possible direction:

A homepage built around Capability Graph, Thinking Lab, Currently Building, and flagship case studies.

---

### 5. AI Documentation Workflow

Status:

Exploring

Problem:

Project knowledge is often lost when implementation moves quickly.

Why it matters:

Documentation helps preserve reasoning, decisions, and future handoff context.

Possible direction:

An AI-assisted workflow that creates changelogs, project notes, and implementation summaries after each development session.

---

### 6. Investment Analytics Dashboard

Status:

Existing project / future improvement

Problem:

Market decisions can become emotional without structured tracking.

Why it matters:

A dashboard can help separate portfolio allocation, market context, and decision review.

Possible direction:

A personal investing dashboard that tracks allocation, watchlists, assumptions, and review notes.

---

### 7. AI Agent Orchestration for Business Systems

Status:

Future exploration

Problem:

Many business ideas may soon be built faster by coordinating specialized AI agents.

Why it matters:

The key value will not only be coding. It will be problem framing, system design, validation, and explanation.

Possible direction:

A workflow where AI agents support research, SQL, coding, writing, and design, while human judgment controls the business logic and final decision.

---

## Recommended Homepage Limit

Do not show too many ideas on the homepage.

Recommended visible limit:

- 5 to 8 idea cards maximum

If more ideas exist, create a separate deeper archive later.

---

## Interaction Direction

Possible interaction:

- Filter by status: Active, Exploring, Prototype, Future
- Click card to expand Problem / Why it matters / Possible direction
- Keep all cards readable without JavaScript

Avoid:

- Heavy animations
- Overly complex idea database
- Making unfinished ideas look like completed work

---

## Success Criteria

The visitor should think:

> This person notices problems and thinks in systems.

Not:

> This person has many unfinished projects.

The section succeeds only if ideas are curated, grounded, and clearly framed as exploration.
