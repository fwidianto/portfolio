# Homepage Thinking Lab

Status: Active source of truth

This document defines the Thinking Lab concept for the homepage. It follows the philosophy in [../core/portfolio-design-philosophy.md](../core/portfolio-design-philosophy.md).

## Purpose

Thinking Lab should show current thinking, not unfinished work.

Its purpose is to reveal curiosity, active learning, and system-design habits through carefully selected notes.

## What It May Contain

Thinking Lab may include:

- questions being explored,
- business-system ideas,
- process observations,
- experiments,
- systems being designed,
- current learning notes,
- AI-assisted workflow observations.

## What It Should Demonstrate

The section should help visitors see how Fauzan frames problems before building:

```text
question
-> business context
-> system implication
-> possible experiment
-> validation need
```

It should make thinking visible without turning the homepage into a notebook dump.

## Quality Bar

Thinking Lab entries should be curated.

Avoid:

- random notes,
- unfinished scraps,
- private or confidential business details,
- vague inspiration quotes,
- technology-first experiments with no business question,
- anything that makes the portfolio feel disorganized.

## Relationship to Currently Building

Thinking Lab explains active thinking. Currently Building explains active work.

The two sections may reference each other, but they should not duplicate the same content.

## Implementation Boundary

This document defines the concept only. Do not build a Thinking Lab UI until a future implementation task explicitly requests it.
