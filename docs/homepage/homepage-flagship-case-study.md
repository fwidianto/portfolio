# Homepage Flagship Case Study

Status: Active source of truth

This document defines how the homepage should route visitors toward the flagship case study.

## Flagship Case

The Odoo ERP Analytics case study should remain the strongest proof of the portfolio philosophy.

The canonical case-study planning document is [../case-studies/odoo-erp-analytics.md](../case-studies/odoo-erp-analytics.md).

## Why It Matters

The case study demonstrates:

- business understanding,
- ERP process understanding,
- data modeling,
- dashboard thinking,
- operational traceability,
- AI-assisted implementation,
- validation and human business judgment.

This is stronger than a generic project card because it can show the full reasoning chain:

```text
Business problem
-> Process understanding
-> Business logic
-> Technology
-> Evidence
```

## Homepage Role

The homepage should guide visitors toward this case study after they have seen:

- the Business Systems Designer identity,
- the Capability Graph,
- active thinking through Thinking Lab,
- evidence that the portfolio is actively maintained.

The homepage should not rewrite the full case study. It should frame why the case matters and make the next click obvious.

## Implementation Boundary

This document defines routing and narrative priority only. It does not specify UI layout or interaction details.
