## Case-Study Story Order

Every case study should eventually follow:

```text
Business problem
-> Process understanding
-> Business logic
-> Technology
-> Evidence
```

Technology supports the story. It is not the story.

## What Belongs Here

- Case-study structure.
- Public-safe evidence planning.
- Project-specific reasoning.
- Notes that help future ChatGPT or Codex sessions preserve the business-first narrative.

## What Should Not Be Placed Here

- Homepage section concepts.
- General visual design direction.
- Private business details.
- Implementation-only task notes.
