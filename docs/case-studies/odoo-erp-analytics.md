# Odoo ERP Analytics Case Study

Status: Active planning source

This document defines the future structure of the Odoo ERP Analytics case study. It is a placeholder until the full public case study is ready.

The case study should be the strongest evidence for the portfolio direction: **Business Systems Designer**.

## Purpose

The case should show how business understanding, ERP process understanding, data modeling, dashboard thinking, operational traceability, AI-assisted implementation, validation, and human judgment combine into a practical solution.

Avoid making the case study purely technical.

## Future Structure

1. Business context
2. Problem
3. Process understanding
4. System/data model
5. Business logic
6. Solution
7. Validation
8. Evidence
9. What this demonstrates about Fauzan's thinking

## Narrative Rule

Use this story order:

```text
Business problem
-> Process understanding
-> Business logic
-> Technology
-> Evidence
```

Do not start with tools. Start with the operational question that made the tools useful.

## Evidence to Preserve

Future versions should preserve public-safe evidence of:

- ERP process interpretation,
- data-model reasoning,
- dashboard logic,
- traceability from business question to metric,
- validation steps,
- decisions made by human judgment,
- where AI-assisted development accelerated execution.
