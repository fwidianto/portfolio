# Target Audience

## Primary audience

The primary audience for this portfolio is experienced HR, recruiters, hiring managers, business managers, and functional leaders who are likely to evaluate the portfolio from a professional hiring perspective.

Expected audience profile:

- Age range: approximately 30 to 50 years old.
- Experience level: experienced professionals, not entry-level screeners only.
- Likely roles: HR, recruiter, hiring manager, operations manager, business manager, analytics manager, or functional stakeholder.
- Evaluation style: practical, business-oriented, time-constrained, and evidence-driven.

## Design implication

The portfolio should not feel like a playful developer showcase or a student portfolio. It should feel mature, calm, readable, and business-relevant.

Visitors should be able to quickly understand:

1. What kind of professional Fauzan is.
2. How his capabilities developed.
3. Why his projects are relevant to business problems.
4. How he thinks, validates, and builds systems.
5. Which evidence supports the positioning.

## Communication principle

Use language that is clear for experienced business readers:

- Prefer business meaning over technical novelty.
- Prefer evidence over self-praise.
- Prefer structured explanation over buzzwords.
- Prefer calm confidence over flashy presentation.
- Keep AI positioned as an execution accelerator, not the main identity.

## Homepage implication

The homepage should guide the visitor through a professional discovery path:

Identity → Capability Growth → Business Systems Thinking → Flagship Evidence → Supporting Projects

The Capability Growth Curve is acceptable because it helps experienced HR and managers understand accumulated capability quickly. It must remain an illustrative maturity metaphor, not a quantified skill score or self-rating chart.
