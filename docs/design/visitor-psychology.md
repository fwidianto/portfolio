# Visitor Psychology

Status: Active source of truth

This document defines what visitors should experience when they enter the portfolio.

## Desired Feeling

Visitors should feel calm confidence, curiosity, and increasing trust.

The portfolio should feel like an experience, not a resume. It should guide visitors through how Fauzan thinks, then give them evidence.

## What Visitors Should Discover

Visitors should discover that Fauzan:

- understands business problems before tools,
- can connect process, data, and decisions,
- learns from real operational contexts,
- builds practical systems,
- uses AI thoughtfully,
- keeps improving the portfolio as a living system.

## What Should Be Subtle

Some points should be implied through structure and evidence rather than repeated:

- curiosity,
- learning speed,
- technical capability,
- AI fluency,
- system-design habits.

## What to Avoid

Avoid:

- resume-style self-description,
- noisy project-gallery aesthetics,
- inflated claims,
- tool-first storytelling,
- making AI the identity,
- visual complexity that hides the business logic.

## Design Implication

The homepage should be designed around visitor discovery. Each interaction and section should make the next idea clearer.
