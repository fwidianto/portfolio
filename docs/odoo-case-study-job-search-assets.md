## Interview answer: “Tell me about your Odoo project”

In my Odoo ERP Analytics project, the main problem was that business data existed across many ERP records, but it was difficult to review as one operational story. One order could involve sales, internal orders, procurement, production, delivery, invoicing, payment, and profitability context.

My role was to map the real business process first, then review how the ERP records connected through fields, statuses, references, and custom logic. After that, I translated the validated logic into dashboard views that could help teams review order progress, delivery status, invoice status, payment follow-up, issue signals, and profitability context.

The important part was not only building a dashboard. It was making sure the dashboard logic reflected the real process. I used AI to speed up coding, debugging, and documentation, but the business-rule validation and interpretation remained human-led.

This project is a good example of how I work: I combine operations knowledge, ERP understanding, data logic, and practical dashboard thinking to make messy business processes easier to review.
