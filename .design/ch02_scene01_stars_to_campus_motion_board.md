# Chapter 02 — Mini-Phase 01: UI Transformation (From Stars to Campus)
## Motion Composition & Art Direction Specification

**Status:** OpenDesign Motion Board & Composition Reference  
**Scene:** Chapter 02, Scene 01 (2014)  
**Dominant Visual Narrative:** `Cosmic Matter → Academic Geometry → Cohort Assembly → Mechanical Engineering Subset (~11) Resolution → Settled University Context`

---

## 1. Visual Storytelling Intent
Per `ANIMATION_REFERENCE_GUIDE.md` and `01_UI_TRANSFORMATION_FROM_STARS_TO_CAMPUS.png`:
- The Red Dwarf energy from Chapter 01 survives the Hero exit via a continuous warm carrier line.
- The astronomical matter gradually loses chaotic cosmic behavior and undergoes an ontological phase shift into disciplined academic geometry.
- Coordinate axes and concentric orbital curves assemble.
- An orderly array of approximately 70 International Program engineering candidates coalesces.
- The ~11 Mechanical Engineering subset illuminates with warm amber/orange prominence and connects through subtle kinematic vector lines.
- The scene quietly settles into minimal context: `2014 · Mechanical Engineering · Universitas Indonesia — International Program`.

---

## 2. Text Budget & Anti-Patterns
- **Visible text:** Strictly limited to year/place eyebrow, single-line headline, one concise contextual sentence, and settled university badge.
- **Forbidden:** No card boxes, no dashboard framing, no telemetry readout panels, no terminal monospacing, no lengthy paragraphs, no decorative UI chrome.
- **Pacing:** One visual idea carried predominantly by motion.

---

## 3. Five Choreographic Beats

| Beat | Window | Visual Transformation | Atmosphere & Elements |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Beat 1: Hero Signal Entry** | 0.0s – 1.5s | Warm amber carrier line flows into the stage; ~120 particles drift with cosmic momentum. | Deep charcoal sky (`#0b0d12`), sparse glowing embers, gentle gravitational deceleration. |
| **Beat 2: Cosmic → Academic Geometry** | 1.5s – 3.5s | Particles organize along concentric orbital ellipses and coordinate grid tracks. | Geometry lines draw in fine hairline amber/white strokes (`rgba(255, 170, 112, 0.25)`). Matter becomes ordered. |
| **Beat 3: Cohort Formation** | 3.5s – 5.5s | An organized field of ~70 student nodes emerges across the geometric matrix. | Muted ivory stardust dots (`rgba(220, 226, 235, 0.5)`), breathing in unison. |
| **Beat 4: Mechanical Subset Resolution** | 5.5s – 7.5s | ~11 central nodes ignite with intense warm amber glow (`#ff6b35`). Delicate kinematic linkage vectors draw between them. | The ~59 broader engineering nodes recede to subtle background. Kinematic lines indicate physical mechanism/linkage. |
| **Beat 5: University Context Settle** | 7.5s – 9.0s | Official Universitas Indonesia Makara seal appears as a soft, respectful background watermark; typography settles quietly. | Restrained, mature editorial closure: `2014 · Mechanical Engineering · Universitas Indonesia — International Program`. |
