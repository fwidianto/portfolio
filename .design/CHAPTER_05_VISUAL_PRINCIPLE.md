# Six-aspect design definition

## 1. Purpose

Show the evolution from operating / analyzing a business system into **orchestrating work across that system**, including the use of AI agents as practical execution support.

The message is not “I use AI.”

The message is:

> **I increasingly direct, delegate, inspect, and synthesize work across an operating system.**

AI is a capability inside the system, not the identity of the chapter.

## 2. Audience

Hiring managers and operational leaders in:

- Business / Operations Analytics
- Process Excellence
- Strategy & Operations
- BI
- Business Systems / ERP
- Operational Intelligence

The chapter must remain understandable without knowing Odoo, agents, LLMs, or AI tooling.

## 3. Information hierarchy

**Primary:** the business operating process.

**Secondary:** operational visibility / control.

**Tertiary:** AI activity.

The main canvas must never visually rank AI above the business process.

## 4. Visual character

Continue the established dark astronomical / editorial systems language.

Required character:

- near-black / charcoal field
- sparse familiar starfield
- restrained warm amber for the human / business operating system
- cyan may appear only as a temporary computational / agent signal when semantically earned
- thin precise paths
- generous negative space
- calm settled state
- minimal text
- motion carries most of the explanation

Do not introduce a new “AI visual language.”

## 5. Composition direction

The primary composition is a single clean horizontal operational spine:

`Estimate / RKB -> Quotation -> Sales Order -> Fulfilment -> Delivery -> Invoice -> Payment`

This is the visible business system.

A small number of analytical / control relationships may appear temporarily around it, but the settled frame stays extremely simple.

Chapter V may retain the underlying **Control Tower × Process X-Ray** idea, but the x-ray / intelligence layers should appear through animation and inspection rather than as permanently visible UI.

## 6. Signature idea

### **Invisible Orchestration**

The viewer first sees a clean operational system.

Only through motion do they discover that work is being delegated, executed in parallel, checked, synthesized, and returned into the operating flow.

AI is **implied by behavior**, not announced by labels or iconography.

---

# Exact visual principle

> **The operating system becomes increasingly intelligent while the interface becomes increasingly quiet.**

AI must not become the visual subject.

The visual subject is the operating system and the owner's increasing ability to orchestrate it.

The settled frame should be simple enough that it could initially be mistaken for a refined operating-process map.

The intelligence reveals itself only when the system moves.

---

# Signature motion behavior

A normal warm amber signal travels along the operating spine.

When a sufficiently complex task reaches a selected stage — likely `Sales Order`, `Fulfilment`, or another semantically appropriate point:

1. the amber signal reaches the stage normally;
2. a restrained secondary signal leaves the main spine;
3. the secondary signal shifts subtly toward cyan;
4. it splits into roughly **3–4 parallel trajectories**;
5. the trajectories execute different behaviors with different timing:
   - one may travel directly;
   - one may inspect an adjacent relationship;
   - one may wait for another result;
   - one may loop / reject once before resolving;
6. the trajectories reconverge;
7. the result returns to the main spine;
8. the returned signal resolves back into the warm amber business flow;
9. temporary agent traces fade away;
10. the business process continues.

The motion should communicate, without explanatory text:

`delegate -> parallel execution -> review -> synthesis -> decision`

Do **not** label these steps on the main canvas.

The paths must be causal and continuous. No teleporting.

---

# Human / AI relationship

Do not place a giant “YOU” node, human portrait, brain, robot, chatbot, or glowing AI core in the center.

The owner's presence should be inferred through orchestration:

- a subtle control point directs work;
- parallel work returns to it or to the relevant business stage;
- the result is accepted back into the primary operating flow.

The story is **human direction + machine amplification**, not autonomous AI replacing the operator.

---

# Chapter IV -> Chapter V transition

If Chapter IV ends with an operating spine similar to:

`Customer -> Quote -> Procure -> Make -> Deliver -> Bill -> Collect`

do not cut to an unrelated composition.

The same spine should conceptually persist and become more precise / digital:

`Estimate / RKB -> Quotation -> Sales Order -> Fulfilment -> Delivery -> Invoice -> Payment`

Then, for the first time, one signal branches away from the spine and performs parallel work.

That is the moment the viewer discovers AI / agent orchestration.

No explicit “NOW AI” title is required.

---

# Process X-Ray behavior

Operational visibility still matters, but keep it subordinate to the primary spine.

During selected moments or post-animation inspection, a stage may temporarily expose only its relevant hidden relationships.

Examples:

- Sales Order -> Procurement / Stock
- Fulfilment -> MO / Stock -> Delivery
- Delivery -> Invoice
- Invoice -> Payment
- Profitability -> contributing process records

These should be temporary thin traces, not a permanent dense graph.

After inspection, the composition should return to calm.

---

# Post-animation behavior

After the automatic sequence settles:

- hovering / focusing a stage may reveal its relevant hidden relationships;
- a dormant orchestration point may quietly activate on focus;
- no cards should automatically appear;
- no permanent agent swarm;
- no large dashboard;
- no scrolling text reel;
- deeper evidence can be handled later through a separate inspection mechanism.

The default resting state must remain visually quiet.

---

# Settled-state rule

At the end of the chapter, most temporary complexity disappears.

Final composition:

- one clean operational spine;
- a few restrained analytical relationships at most;
- perhaps one subtle dormant orchestration branch point;
- sparse starfield;
- large negative space.

The final visual statement is:

> **AI is infrastructure, not decoration.**

# Hard minimalism constraints

These are mandatory for visual exploration.

## Do not use

- explanatory paragraphs on canvas
- slogans / marketing copy
- feature lists
- legends unless absolutely necessary
- “AI AGENT” labels everywhere
- agent names as permanent UI
- robot icons
- brain icons as the central metaphor
- chatbot bubbles
- glowing AI core
- giant “YOU” node
- dense node graphs
- orbit-ring spectacle
- multiple dashboards
- cards / panels
- generic SaaS UI
- glassmorphism
- decorative gradients
- cyberpunk / gaming visual language
- excessive glow
- decorative motion without causal meaning
- unnecessary icons
- repeated labels
- persistent cyan everywhere

## Main-canvas text target

As close to zero as the composition allows.

Essential process-stage labels are acceptable.

Everything else should be expressed through hierarchy, geometry, and motion.

---

# Color semantics

Do not treat exact color values as frozen yet.

Semantic rule only:

- **warm amber / bronze** = primary human/business operating flow
- **cyan** = temporary computation / delegated agent activity
- **warm off-white** = essential typography
- **near-black** = environment

Cyan should recede after agent work completes.
