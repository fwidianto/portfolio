# Study A — Technical Assembly

## Visual thesis

**The academic apparatus retires; the continuity signal begins drawing the geometry of the professional industrial world.**

## Keyframes

### A0 — Thesis hold
Approved thesis apparatus remains readable and calm.

### A1 — Release
The warm continuity signal separates from the apparatus. Non-essential apparatus lines soften into the background without collapsing theatrically.

### A2 — Draft path
The signal travels across negative space while a few fine drafting guides appear ahead of it.

### A3 — Industrial construction
As the signal reaches the destination, restrained technical linework assembles:

- service-machine silhouette;
- service access point;
- technician position;
- component / spare-part position.

### A4 — Arrival hold
The new industrial technical plate is complete enough to read. Traktor Nusantara / 2019 appears subtly.

## Strength

Clear, controlled, easy to read.

## Risk

Could feel like a conventional blueprint animation if overused.

# Study B — Depth Reveal

## Visual thesis

**The professional industrial world already exists beyond the academic frame; crossing into it reveals a deeper layer rather than constructing a new slide.**

## Keyframes

### B0 — Thesis hold
Apparatus remains calm.

### B1 — Depth separation
The apparatus gradually recedes in contrast / optical depth while the continuity signal moves forward.

### B2 — Crossing
The signal travels through a dark spatial threshold. Faint industrial silhouettes are already present but nearly invisible.

### B3 — Reveal
As the signal arrives, local depth / restrained light reveals:

- machine silhouette;
- technician position;
- component position;
- structural floor / service context.

### B4 — Arrival hold
The industrial world sits confidently in space, with Traktor Nusantara / 2019 appearing as a quiet identity cue.

## Strength

Cinematic and mature.

## Risk

Can become too atmospheric and insufficiently specific if the industrial geometry is weak.

# Study C — Material Transformation

## Visual thesis

**The geometry of engineering education does not disappear; selected structural lines transform into the geometry of professional industrial work.**

This was an explored alternative but is not the owner-selected direction.

## Keyframes

### C0 — Thesis hold
The final apparatus remains intact and calm.

### C1 — Structural extraction
The warm continuity signal activates only a small set of transferable geometric relationships from the apparatus:

- horizontal baseline;
- vertical service / pipe-like axis;
- one rotating / circular mechanical motif;
- one component / vessel-like mass;
- one measurement / connection point.

The full apparatus does not melt or morph.

### C2 — Re-routing
Those selected structural lines detach from their old functional meaning and are gently re-routed through negative space with the warm signal.

The viewer should be able to follow where the geometry came from.

### C3 — Recomposition
The transferred lines settle into a new arrangement and begin reading as:

- chassis / machine baseline;
- service access geometry;
- wheel / rotating mechanical element if appropriate;
- technician working zone;
- spare-part / component zone.

New industrial geometry may be added only after the inherited lines establish the new composition.

### C4 — Identity shift
The remaining thesis-specific geometry fully recedes.

A restrained Traktor Nusantara / 2019 cue appears.

### C5 — Arrival hold
The new industrial service plate is stable.

The continuity signal rests at or near the future service action point, ready for Beat 03.

## Strength

Most personal and conceptually continuous.

It says:

**the subject changed, but the engineering way of seeing continued.**

## Risk

If too much geometry morphs simultaneously it will feel gimmicky, magical, or like a logo animation.

Therefore the transformation must use only a **small subset of structural lines**, not transform the entire thesis apparatus into a machine.
