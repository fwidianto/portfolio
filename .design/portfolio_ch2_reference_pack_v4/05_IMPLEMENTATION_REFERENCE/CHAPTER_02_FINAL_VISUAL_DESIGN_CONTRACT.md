# Chapter 02 — Final Visual Design Contract (Pre-Implementation)

**Status:** Owner review candidate  
**Scope:** exact visual composition and motion intent only; no frontend implementation authorization.

## Global design

- Direction: **Dark Editorial Engineering**.
- Maintain the dark Red Dwarf DNA, but reduce literal astronomy scene by scene.
- Use one thin warm red-orange signal line as the connective device.
- Photography is evidence, not decoration. Use real photos only when they add place, proof, or human reality.
- Core understanding must remain scroll-readable without interaction.

## Scene 01 — Entering the International Program

**Composition:** concise two-column opening. Left: 2014 / Mechanical Engineering / Universitas Indonesia — International Program. Right: cohort visualization resolving roughly 70 Engineering International students into the smaller ~11 Mechanical Engineering group.

**Motion:** the Hero signal enters first. Diffuse star-like points lose their cosmic behavior and settle into a clean academic cohort grid. The UI/Makara cue should be restrained and secondary.

**Scroll target:** about one viewport. Keep this scene short.

## Scene 02 — Finding My Footing

**Composition:** a central `observe → calculate → test → adjust` loop, surrounded by only five selected technical fragments: Thermodynamics, Measurement/Metrology, Control Systems, CAD, and Statistics.

**Motion:** reveal one fragment at a time. Each fragment briefly feeds the central loop and recedes. No large photograph is necessary. The scene exits when the warm signal straightens from an academic trace into a process line.

**Scroll target:** approximately 1.1–1.3 viewports.

## Scene 03 — 2018: Astra → Tokyo

This remains the chapter centerpiece and longest scene.

The mill geometry simplifies until only one warm line remains. That same line crosses the viewport and becomes part of a cleaner Tokyo academic/urban grid. Do not use a default airplane/travel animation.

### Tokyo

## Scene 04 — Coming Back and Finishing

**Composition:** technical workbench. Left: authentic throttling-system diagram. Center: selected variables. Right: authentic experiment setup. One restrained graph/result layer may appear after the experiment is established.

**Motion sequence:** `system setup → variables → simulation → experiment → result`.

**Close:** settle quietly into `Universitas Indonesia · Mechanical Engineering · 2014–2019`. No victory animation.

**Scroll target:** approximately 1.3–1.5 viewports.

## Responsive behavior

Desktop may use asymmetric horizontal compositions. Tablet collapses evidence into paired blocks. Mobile preserves the same story order vertically; process lines become vertical traces and no essential meaning depends on hover.

## Acceptance boundary

Owner review should judge only: composition, hierarchy, evidence placement, photo role, and motion intent. Once accepted, convert this into an implementation contract. Do **not** start Chapter 02 frontend work until the current Hero implementation gate is closed.
